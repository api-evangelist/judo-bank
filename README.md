Judo Bank (Judo Capital Holdings Ltd) is an Australian challenger bank founded in 2016 and headquartered in Melbourne, purpose-built to serve small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) with relationship-led business lending alongside personal and business term deposits. It was the first new domestically-owned bank in decades to be granted a full, unrestricted authorised deposit-taking institution (ADI) licence by APRA, in April 2019, and has been publicly listed on the Australian Securities Exchange (ticker ASX JDO) since November 2021 - it is a for-profit, shareholder-owned bank, not a customer-owned mutual. As a designated ADI and data holder under Australia's Consumer Data Right (CDR / Open Banking) regime, Judo Bank exposes a public, unauthenticated Product Reference Data (PRD) API conforming to the Data Standards Body (DSB) Consumer Data Standards, while authenticated consumer-data sharing is governed by the accredited data recipient (ADR) model rather than an open self-serve developer program.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Judo Bank: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
